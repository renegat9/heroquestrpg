# Équipement et armurerie

> Extrait de `CLAUDE.md` le 2026-09-06, **verbatim**. Ce sont les **règles en
> vigueur** — pas un document daté comme `docs/plan-*.md` / `docs/verdict-*.md`.
> Index : `CLAUDE.md` §« Règles établies ».

**Equipment mastery (who may wear what).** Each weapon/armour row carries a `tag_equipement`; each class a base `tags_equipement` (`classes_heros`); and `deblocage` skill nodes whose effect is `{mecanique: acces_equipement, tags: […]}` add more. `Equipement::verifierAccesEquipement()` is the single gate — both the hub route and the in-quest equip action go through it. Tags now translate the deck's per-class restrictions one for one (doc 16 §2.2), eleven of them plus four `talisman_*`. Profile is **canon HeroQuest**: the **magicien** is limited to light and scholar weapons and wears no *ordinary* armour — lifted by *Cuir d'apprenti* / *Escrime de fortune* — but he is the **only** one who may wear bracers and the cloak of protection (`armure_magicien`), his first armour ever; and the two bruisers **both get everything from level 1** (René, 2026-08-22): barbare and nain each wear plate *and* swing two-handed weapons, no node to buy. They used to be **mirrored** — each specialty free, the other's paid for with a skill point (*Maîtrise lourde*, *Poigne de forgeron*) — and both nodes are now **deleted**. The elfe still gets neither. ⚠ Removing them from `CompetenceSeeder` was not enough: it uses `updateOrCreate` and deliberately never purges (a purge would detach nodes heroes already own), so a migration deletes the rows and their acquisitions — otherwise the tree would keep offering a node that opens nothing. ⚠ **Known debt**: this leaves the barbare alone at **4** tree nodes where every other class has 5 or more; `MonteeNiveauTest` records it at its floor rather than hiding it. ⚠ **Two identical copies are not an offer**: `Equipement::equiper()` auto-swaps, so a second helmet in the bag produced « Équiper Casque » directly under « Ranger Casque » — a menu contradicting itself, for a gesture that costs the turn's action and changes nothing (René, 2026-09-04). `Equipement::echangeUtile()` is the single point of passage — the in-quest menu drops the slot, `/moi` publishes `slots_utiles` and `remplace` so the hub bag reads the same decision instead of re-deriving it client-side, and an occupied slot now **names what it replaces** rather than showing a second identical label. ⚠ Equivalence is objet **and** Forge `ameliorations`: two « Épée large » stop being interchangeable the moment one is upgraded, and hiding that swap would cost the player the better one. `deux_mains` is **orthogonal** to the tag — it only forbids a shield, so the two-handed *Bâton* (whose card states no class restriction) stays the magicien's best weapon. A class with no declared tags means **no restriction** (fail open): missing reference data must never lock a hero out of their own starting gear. The check runs only when equipping — already-worn pieces are never stripped retroactively. This finished a design that had been declared in `CompetenceSeeder` since day one and never read: the engine used to test a hardcoded `necessite_maitrise_lourde` flag instead, so no class had any real equipment limit.

**The class CARD BACKS are now data** (René, 2026-08-22). Ten printed rules that no other data could imply — source facts, like the monster stat line. Two of them needed new columns, and both for the same reason: a fact was being inferred from another that merely overlaps it today.

⚠ **`objets.metallique`** — Bard, Druid and Rogue reason about *metal* armour, while the mastery tags say *weight* (`armure_legere`/`armure_lourde`). The catalogue holds only two ordinary armours and both are metal, so deriving it would work right now and break silently the day a leather armour is added — the Bard would lose his die with nothing to say so. Flagged on chain mail, plate, helm **and the Armour of Borin** (artefact, René's reminder). ⚠ The **shield is NOT flagged**: the cards name it separately from metal, and flagging it would quietly take the Druid's shield, which they never forbid him.

⚠ **`classes_heros.objets_autorises`** — the Monk wields only dagger, crossbow, handaxe, shortsword and staff. No tag combination says that: handaxe and shortsword share `arme_courante` with the broadsword, longsword and rapier he may not touch. A per-class whitelist that **replaces** the tag check when present, and applies only to pieces that demand a mastery — a potion is not a weapon. Re-tagging the five would have broken ten classes to serve one, `tag_equipement` being a single column.

The rest reuses what existed: **Berserker** loses `arme_distance`; **Rogue** loses his armour tag and starts with the **Bandolier** (which carries `compte_comme_arme: Dague`, making his Ambidextrie literal from turn one without occupying his off-hand); **Warlock** is aligned on the wizard's tags plus his own wand; **Knight** gets `Equipement::malusDeplacement()`, a single reader both `MenuMoteur` and `ResolveurTour` call — two exemptions to keep in step would have drifted, and the menu would offer squares the resolver refuses. ⚠ **Dwarf and Explorer disarm without tools**, and that is a *different resolution*, not a bonus: one die, and only a **black shield** fails. Giving them the ordinary Body check would have emptied the printed rule of its substance. The Bard's `bonus_des_defense_sans_metal` was already fully wired — only its notion of "metal" moved to the new column.

**The armoury is a CONVERSION of an equipment-card deck** (`reference/16_armurerie.md` §2.2, keywords in doc 19 §9). A physical card states its effect in one sentence — "This weapon allows you to attack diagonally", "Two-handed", "you have a 2 square movement penalty". Here that effect is *data*, so each sentence becomes stats (price, dice) plus keywords from a closed vocabulary, `App\Engine\MotsClesEquipement`. Doc 16 carries **two source tiers** that must stay apart: **§2.1 the booklets** (what the rest of the doc is opposable on — no price, almost no dice count), **§2.2 the deck**. The deck we port is **Sjeng / Ye Olde Inn** (`sjeng-equipment.pdf`, 27 cards, René's pick 2026-08-08) — and it says so on its own page 5: *"I have changed some item costs and functionality"*. ⚠ **The catalogue IS the official deck since 2026-08-15** — René's photos of the real Hasbro cards (20 equipment + 15 potions, each stamped © 2021/2022/2023) are transcribed card by card in **§2.1bis**, from the project's Drive folder [`1seESGzXRhVw7ijIPuRVisaE36BPPoJ53`](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1seESGzXRhVw7ijIPuRVisaE36BPPoJ53) (public link, readable over plain HTTP via `embeddedfolderview` — the Drive MCP connector is *not* authenticated here). §2.1bis **outranks §2.2**, which is now a historical annex, and it settled the oldest dead end in the doc: prices do exist. **Twelve pieces left the catalogue** — cane, sling, whip, both bows, spear, halberd, mace, flail, greatsword, bastard sword, cloak of protection: the fan deck invented them, and `ArmurerieConformeTest`'s list of "off-source" pieces, seventeen names long, is now **empty** and must stay so. Ten were repriced (dagger **150 → 25**, bracers **200 → 550**), the **staff fell from 2 dice to 1** — its card's number, which costs the magicien his best weapon — and the **bracers turned out to have no class restriction at all** where we reserved them to the wizard. Three of our own claims were falsified on the way: the *Handaxe*, *Battle Axe* and *Chain Mail* all have official cards, where §2.1 and a `GroupeController` comment said the hatchet "exists nowhere in the official material"; only the **spear** is still untraceable.

**Nineteen items entered with a reader each** (the condition René set: keep the cards *and* make them work). Four are hardware — **caltrops** (a runtime `carte.grille['chausse_trappes']` layer, one combat die on entry, white shield to keep going), **smoke bomb** (the monster leaves `$occupees`, which lifts movement blocking AND line of sight in one gesture, since that set is the engine's only occupancy loop — but ⚠ *traversing is not stopping*: ending a move on its square is refused), **holy water** (kills undead by `monstres.nom_base`, never the AI-dressed name), **bandolier** (`compte_comme_arme`: no extra die, only the rules that *require* a dagger — the Rogue's Ambidextrie, and closing the Monk's bare-handed techniques). Fifteen are potions, and three of them carry a **class restriction — a first for a consumable**: `MoteurPotions` consulted no right at all, since a consumable never goes through `equiper()`. The decision is extracted into `Equipement::estAccessible()` and applied at the **three** places that must agree — the resolver *refuses*, `/moi` *badges* (`utilisable`, greying the button — a hero may carry a companion's potion), `MoteurReactions::soinsDisponibles()` *filters* (offering an emergency heal the resolver would refuse is worse than offering none).

Twelve keywords were added to `MotsClesEquipement`, each with its reader, plus a **seventh duration**: `plus_de_monstre_en_vue`, which the barbarian's two potions need and `fin_du_combat` cannot say — the latter reasons over the whole quest, the former over the bearer's **line of sight**. ⚠ It is evaluated at the **start of the turn** (`MoteurSorts::rythmerBuffsDeVue()`, same hook as the Monk's style recovery, same idempotence guard), which both expires the buffs *and* **re-arms** Battle Rage's second attack — without that guard, re-arming after every action would grant a third attack.

**Two weapons in hand — the off-hand grants a CHOICE, not a die** (René's call, 2026-08-12; doc 16 §2.2, doc 01 §7). Four loadouts, and only four: two one-handed weapons · one two-handed weapon · one one-handed weapon + shield · one one-handed weapon alone. This had been blocked twice over, and the second blocker was the real one: *no card says what the second weapon grants*, so wiring it meant inventing the one number that matters. René's answer removes the number entirely — the off-hand adds **nothing** to the dice, it adds an attack **option**. The menu therefore emits one attack per weapon ("Attaquer — Épée large", "Attaquer — Dague"), each carrying **its own** legal targets, because reach, diagonals and throwing belong to the WEAPON, not the hero: a crossbow sees the room, a sword its four neighbours, a longsword adds the diagonals.

The first blocker is gone too: `Equipement::slotsPossibles()` returns **two** slots for a one-handed weapon, so the slot becomes a parameter — of the API (`POST /equipement {emplacement?}`), of the in-quest menu (`equiper_{id}` / `equiper_{id}_gauche`) and of the controller (two buttons). ⚠ Dice are read **per weapon**: the `des_attaque` column stays the reference — it carries class, main-hand weapon, Forge and passive nodes — and striking left-handed swaps only the *weapon's share* of it (`desAttaqueAvec()`); recomputing the whole thing would drop everything else on the floor. `frapper()` takes the inventory line used, and with it the Vindication arrow, the thrown weapon (that one is the one lost) and the targeting keywords. Two intended side effects: the Rogue's **Ambidextrie becomes literal** ("one additional attack *with a dagger*" — that's why René wanted this), and **"unarmed" now looks at BOTH hands**, so a dagger on the left closes the Monk's bare-handed techniques — a shield does not, it never strikes (`Equipement::armesEnMain()`).

**Charges and spell economy** (`config/cartes.php`, doc 19 §9, René's call 2026-08-09) — the two mechanics that were blocking seven cards, opened together because three of those cards need both. A **charge** says "this copy has N uses": `inventaire.charges`, not `quantite` (which counts identical copies — a four-arrow bow is one object used four times). `null` means *never touched*, not spent, so every path that creates an inventory row (market, chest, gift, loot) stays ignorant of charges; at zero the piece goes **inert** — it stays in the bag, its effect stops. Nothing is destroyed: an item vanishing on its own would be a surprise, not a rule. `MoteurCharges` is the only reader. **Spell economy** is the pivot `personnage_sorts.disponible`, which was a bare boolean reset per quest, with the only two exceptions being skill *nodes* (Concentration returns one spell, Réserve arcanique grants a second cast) — no object could touch it. Now: `restaure_sorts` (all spells back — the scale is what makes the card worth finding), `second_sort_par_tour` (the node's power on an object; both share `etat.bonus_sort_utilise`, so they don't stack), `sort_non_epuise` (one spell spared for one charge), `sort_non_epuise_sur_bouclier_noir` (a die after each cast, 1-in-6). ⚠ **Order matters**: the rod is rolled *before* the ring is spent — succeeding on a free effect must not burn a scarce one. The Spell Ring diverges deliberately: the card picks the spell at quest start, we pick it by casting — same outcome, no blind bet before seeing the dungeon. The chest also gained a fallback: a **consumable** artefact rather than gold when every portable one is already held, which is what finally gave the Anti-Poison Quill a way into the game (it needed no mechanic at all, only a channel).

**The stall drops what nobody in the party could use** (René's call 2026-08-18 — the same rule as furniture loot). Three potions are barbarian-only and two elf-only; a party with neither is no longer offered them. ⚠ This does **not** close off buying for a companion: the filter uses the **union** of every active member's masteries (`Equipement::tagsAccessiblesAux()`), so a barbarian potion stays on the shelf as long as the party has a barbarian, whoever pays — the purse is shared and gifting exists. What disappears is only what no hero of this party could ever drink or wear, and the "non maîtrisé" badge still tells *this* player what *their* hero cannot equip. Empty mastery list means no restriction (fail open).

**A new campaign starts with 0 gold — on purpose** (René's call, 2026-08-05). Heroes begin with their class weapon and nothing else; the first market phase of a campaign therefore has an empty purse, nothing is affordable and all three mercenaries read "Or insuffisant". That is the intended shape, as on the board: the first loot is earned in the dungeon, and the market only becomes meaningful from quest 2 on. Do **not** "fix" it by granting a starting purse.

**Giving items between heroes.** `POST /groupes/{id}/dons` (`app/Partie/DonObjet.php`) — **hub only**, like equip/unequip and the Forge. A player gives from **their own** heroes to **any active hero** of the group, including another player's; the receiver confirms nothing but their bag capacity is enforced, while the **giver** may be over capacity (shedding an item is how you regularise a bag saturated by quest loot). An equipped piece must be unequipped first. Consumables transfer by stack and **merge**; everything else **moves the inventory row** rather than recreating it — recreating would silently drop the row's Forge `ameliorations`. Artefacts circulate too: a `unique` weapon belongs to the group, and giving is not selling.

⚠ **Un chiffre de fiche officielle est un TOTAL ÉQUIPÉ, une valeur de classe est une BASE** (René, 2026-09-11 : « la carte de départ compte déjà le bouclier dans la fiche »). Une fiche de héros montre le personnage **avec son matériel de départ** ; la valeur à seeder s'en déduit en retirant cet équipement. Le **Chevalier** affiche `D3` et démarre avec un bouclier (+1) : sa base est **2**. Il était seedé à 3 et recevait le bouclier par-dessus — **4 dés de défense dès le premier tour**, le héros le plus dur à blesser du jeu, quand les classes de base plafonnent à 2.

⚠ **C'est le MÊME défaut que celui déjà corrigé côté attaque**, et le docblock d'`Equipement::recalculerCombat()` le racontait déjà : « l'arme s'AJOUTAIT à une valeur de classe qui encodait déjà l'arme de départ — un barbare (3) avec une épée large (3) arrivait à 6 dés ». Le correctif d'alors n'a porté que sur l'**attaque**, et son commentaire affirmait « les quatre classes ont 2 dés de base, **aucun double compte à corriger** ». Vrai à l'écriture, faux dès l'arrivée du Chevalier, et **jamais revu**. Une phrase exacte peut périmer sans qu'une ligne de code bouge : c'est l'arrivée d'une donnée qui l'a rendue fausse.

⚠ Le **Moine** reste à 3 et c'est correct — il ne démarre ni avec un bouclier ni avec une armure, donc son 3 **est** une base. Ce contraste est ce qui rend la règle vérifiable : pour toute classe, `des_defense` seedé + équipement de départ doit **égaler** le chiffre de sa fiche.
