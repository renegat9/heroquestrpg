**Artefacts are a CONVERSION too** (`sjeng-artefacts.pdf`, Ye Olde Inn — 34 cards from five sources; doc 16 §9.1, René's pick 2026-08-09). They replaced **7 artefacts we had invented**, and the reason matters: those were seven weapons that only climbed the dice curve — 4, then 5, then 6. A real artefact doesn't raise the curve, it does something nothing else does. The 6-dice *Fendoir des Titans* made the whole armoury moot the moment you found it; the *Lame des Esprits* that replaces it rolls 3 — except against undead. Nine cards are ported (3 weapons, Borin's Armour, 5 talismans), each with a real reader: `degats_fixes` (no dice, no defence — the only key that bypasses `Engine\Combat`), `des_attaque_contre` (**replaces** the weapon's dice, per the card's "OR"), `attaque_double_contre`, `bonus_pv_body_max` / `bonus_pv_mind_max`. The two "contre" keys test `monstres.nom_base`, the **catalogue** name — never the AI-dressed one, or the blade would stop recognising undead the first time a quest is narrated. Twenty-five cards are **deliberately not seeded**, each listed with the mechanic it needs (spell economy, damage types, charges, teleport…): a card whose central effect the engine ignores is a rule promised and never kept. A fifth slot, `talisman`, carries the four class jewels — they grant max HP, not dice, so neither `armure` nor `casque` fitted without making them compete with real armour. `DeckFouille::choisirArtefact()` now draws weapons **and** armour, and rejects anything no **active class** could ever wear (crossing `tag_equipement` with class tags + `acces_equipement` nodes) — that generalised a hardcoded barbarian check and is what stops a quest's only chest from paying out Dwarven Runestones to a party with no dwarf. Fixing the menu was part of it: `MenuMoteur` gated the whole action block on `! $aAgi`, so **the Potion d'héroïsme's second attack had never been reachable** — the resolver accepted it, but no menu ever offered it and the controller rejects options absent from the last menu.

**The ARTEFACT cards are sourced too, and they emptied five of our own** (René's photos, 2026-09-03 — `artifacts_part1/2.pdf`, 59 cards = **34 distinct artefacts + 19 scroll spells**). Third and last source switch of the week, after the armoury (2026-08-15) and the spells (2026-09-02): the fan pack `sjeng-artefacts.pdf` (Ye Olde Inn) is retired. ⚠ **Five of our artefacts had NO card at all** — Capuche du Magister, Runes naines, Sceptre de Mémoire, Baguette de Galimatias, Parchemin de Sorts — and left the catalogue by migration, because `ObjetSeeder` writes with `updateOrCreate` and never purges, so deleting seeder lines alone would have left them in the DB, still drawn by chests. Their inventory rows go with them: an artefact cut from the catalogue but left in a bag is a row that is neither equippable, sellable nor displayable, and says nothing about it. **One correction on a card we already had**: the *Talisman of Lore* gives **+1** Mind, not +2.

⚠ **A rule outlived its object.** The Sceptre de Mémoire carried `sort_non_epuise_sur_bouclier_noir` alone; deleting it would have left a reader with no data — a decorative key, which this project deletes. René's call: **move the rule onto the `regain_sort` talents** (*Chant runique*, *Appel de la forêt*), where it fixes something else — returning a spell on **every** kill removed the spell economy instead of loosening it, so the regain now needs a **black shield**, one face in six. The keyword therefore leaves `MotsClesEquipement` (no object carries it) and the object branch of `preserverSort()` goes with it rather than querying an item nothing seeds. ⚠ The roll happens **once, before the loop** over exhausted spells: inside, it would be re-rolled per spell, making the brake weaker the emptier the grimoire — the opposite of the intent. Its test stages a **sleeping** monster, which since 2026-09-02 rolls no defence dice: the die queue then reads exactly [3 attack, regain roll], where otherwise the position of the regain die depends on the goblin's defence count and the test measures the catalogue instead of the talent.

⚠ **The ICE box is registered and deliberately NOT ported** (René's call): *Ring of Warmth*, *Armband of Ice*, *Snowshoes of Speed*, and five scroll spells (Chill, Warmth, Ice Storm, Ice Bridge, Skate). Their rules name cold spells, ice vaults and icy rivers that exist nowhere here — it is not a reader they lack, it is **something to resist**. *Warmth* is the honest edge case: a plain 3-point heal, portable as-is, set aside only because it belongs to that box.

⚠ **Scrolls are not objects** — they derive from a spell (`sorts.difficulte_parchemin`), so the 19 cards get their **own registry section**, and `GuideController` counts a scroll as ported when it names a `sort` rather than an `objet`. Eleven already exist; three are sourced but absent from our spell catalogue (**Lightning Bolt**, whose `rayon` path already exists and is the nearest to portable, **Treasure Without Doom**, **Psychic Recovery**). `CartesSourcesTest` now holds 35 artefact cards (34 + the Fire Ring that Kellar's Keep sources elsewhere), **17 ported**, each of the other 18 naming the mechanic it waits on.

**Three artefacts became ACTIVABLE, and they needed no new mechanic** (2026-09-03) — *Poudre d'Invisibilité*, *Cape des Ombres*, *Sceptre de Télékinésie*. Each reunites on an **object** what already existed on **spells**: the dust and the cloak post a movement buff that `traverseRoche()` / `franchitFigures()` re-read from the source item — those readers have always accepted `potion:`-sourced buffs, which is what made the port possible at all — and the rod joins `saute_tour` (Tempête) to the Mind-break roll (Sommeil). ⚠ `MoteurSorts::tenterRupture()` is now **generic over the condition**; `tenterRuptureSommeil()` is a name for the historic use. Two copies of "1 red die per Mind Point, a 6 resists" would have drifted at the first adjustment. ⚠ `activable` is what lets a **non-consumable** enter the "Utiliser un objet" list — the cloak and the rod are worn — and `cible` gives the entry its targets, so **depth follows the data** exactly as for spells: the rod opens a third level, the cloak fires from the second. ⚠ **The charge is the guard rail**: "once per quest" is `charges: 1`, and the menu filters on `disponible()` — an option left standing after the charge is spent is the anti-pattern this project hunts, a button that always answers no. ⚠ A charged artefact **is not consumed**: it goes inert and stays in the bag, so it must not fall through the tail of `resoudreUsageObjet()` that decrements the stack and deletes the row — that tail is written for the three material consumables. ⚠ One displayed condition covers the cloak's **two** mechanics: the readers consult the source **object's** effect, not the condition's, so `franchit_mur` and `franchit_figures` both apply under the single legible label *Intangible*.

**Five more artefacts, still without a new mechanic** (2026-09-03) — the `activable` seam laid for the first three turned out to carry them. *Longue épée de Fortune*: 3 dice + `attaque_diagonale` (both already read) and its once-per-quest reroll posts the **same buff the Potion de bataille posts** (`relance_des_attaque`), the charge expressing the cadence. *Lame Fantôme*: `ignore_defense_monstre` existed as a **talent**; `MoteurSorts::valeurBuff()` — the numeric twin of `aBuff()` — now lets a buff carry it too, read with a `max` and never a sum, since two sources adding up would hand out a negative defence. *Bâton du Magicien*: the only one needing data beyond the item — a **`arme_magicien` tag**, because the wizard carried only `arme_legere`, which three other classes have, so granting the staff through it would have opened it to everyone the card reserves it from. *Élixir de Vie*: `restaure_jauges_depart` already existed on a potion; "brings a dead hero back to life" is rendered as both gauges to max **plus standing the hero up**, our engine having no death — and it is the **only** artefact whose legal target is a hero who is *down*, hence `tombeAdmis` threading from the menu's target list to the resolver. *Bracelet de Guérison*: its second sentence — "if the wearer's Body Points are reduced to 0, use immediately" — **is** the emergency-heal reaction written for potions on 2026-08-13; `soinsDisponibles()` only knew `categorie === 'consommable'`, so worn charged pieces now join the same whitelist under their own `artefact:` key, and the resolution spends a **charge** instead of consuming a copy.

⚠ **What is left needs real new mechanics**, and the registry names each: forcing the *attacker* to reroll (Dawnshield), a die against Dread spells (Scales of Elethorn), intercepting **Mind** damage (Sky Orb — `MoteurDegats` only sees Body), a reroll conditioned on the **face** rolled (Raven's Talon), plus teleport, trap-jumping, spell reflection and monster mind-control. ⚠ And a standing caution when adding more weapon artefacts: the project already discarded seven invented blades because they only climbed the dice curve. Fortune's Longsword escapes the reproach — its reroll is a *power* — but the line is thin.

**Fidelity pass: six artefacts were promising what they did not deliver** (2026-09-03). Re-reading each ported card against its text turned up one cross-cutting confusion and three wrong ports.

⚠ **A charge is a TOTAL, a frequency is a CADENCE, and conflating them cost six artefacts.** `inventaire.charges` says "this copy has N uses" and is **never reset** — `MoteurCharges` only decrements, `DemarreurQuete` never touches it. Expressing "once per quest" with `charges: 1` therefore delivered **once per CAMPAIGN**: Cape des Ombres, Sceptre de Télékinésie, Lame Fantôme, Longue épée de Fortune, Bracelet de Guérison and the Anneau de Sort were all affected. The new `frequence` keyword stores its window in **the competences' own counter** (`etat_personnage_quete.capacites_utilisees`, via `Talents::compteurPour()` / `marquerUtilisee()`): no migration, it dies with the quest, and it already rides the snapshot. ⚠ Charges **stay** for the Arc de Vindication (4 arrows) and the Anneau de Feu (2 uses) — their cards state a finite total, not a cadence. ⚠ `MoteurCharges::utilisable()` is now the single question a menu must ask; `disponible()` alone answered only half of it. ⚠ Its test is the only one in the suite that **plays two quests** — that second half is what proves anything, and it fails on the old model.

⚠ **Longue épée de Fortune** rerolled *all* failed dice where its card says "reroll **1** Attack die": `Combat::resoudreAttaque()`'s reroll flag went from `bool` to a **count**, `PHP_INT_MAX` meaning the whole volley (the barbarian node and the Potion de bataille, whose texts do say the volley). ⚠ **Lame Fantôme** cancelled the target's defence for *any* attack, where the card says "when you attack **with the dagger**": buffs now carry the inventory line that produced them (`potion:{Nom}#{id}`), and `valeurBuffDeLArme()` ignores a buff belonging to another weapon. ⚠ **Arc elfique de Vindication** carried a tag **no class had** — unequippable by anyone, and never even drawn, since `DeckFouille::choisirArtefact()` filters on the group's tags. A test now refuses any orphan tag, so the next dead piece announces itself. ⚠ Both weapon buffs also posted **`Renforcé`**, which means "+attack dice": the player read one thing and got another. Two honest conditions replace it.

⚠ **The poison did nothing at all, on three producers.** `conditions.effet.degats_pv_body_par_tour` sat on *Empoisonné* since the table was created **with no reader anywhere** — two traps and two pieces of furniture told the player they were poisoned and nothing happened. It now bleeds at the end of the bearer's turn, in the same place and for the same reason as the spawnling tokens, through `MoteurDegats::infligerAHeros()` with a new `SOURCE_POISON` (deliberately **not** reactive: one does not cancel a haemorrhage with Dark Wings). ⚠ And `MoteurDegats` now **remembers what hurt you** (René's call): the cumulative total per source and the last blow, on the quest state so it dies with the quest. That is what lets the **Plume anti-poison** be literal — "restores ANY of the owner's Body Points lost by poisoning" — instead of the flat 2 it paid before; the cumulative total is cleared on use so a second quill cannot pay twice.

**Two more cards ported by the same seams**: *Écailles d'Elethorn* needed `MoteurSorts::desResistanceMentale()`, the Mind mirror of `desDefenseHeros()` — `MoteurDread` passed `attribut_mind` **raw** in two places, so no equipment could ever add to a resistance roll; and *Cendres du Phénix* needed `MoteurReactions` to look in the **bag**, its HP-floor offer having only ever consulted skill nodes. It is the first artefact that **destroys itself** on a roll rather than going inert. ⚠ *Rabbit Boots* was left unported on purpose: it needs our jump to widen from a pit to **any discovered trap**, which changes the rule for every hero, not just the wearer — half-porting it is exactly what this pass exists to undo.

⚠ **Testing the rod in real play uncovered a defect far wider than the rod** (2026-09-03). `HabillerMonstres` loads its instances, calls the LLM — seconds to minutes — then writes `habillage` back **from the copy it loaded before the call**. But `habillage` is not only dressing: it carries the monsters' **conditions** (endormi, saute_tour, terrifié, paralysé, ralenti, enfumé), the troll's burn, deferred damage, and `repousse_par`. Everything the party wrote during that window was silently erased — no log line, nothing on screen, and impossible to attribute. It only fires in the first minute of a quest, which is why three campaigns and a thousand tests never saw it: **no worker runs concurrently in a test**. Found by using a Sceptre de Télékinésie right after a quest start and watching `saute_tour` vanish; located by bisection after the menu, the boss and the second action had each been cleared by experiment. The fix is one `refresh()` before the merge. ⚠ Its regression test makes the race **deterministic** by posting the condition from inside the `Http::fake` **callback** — that callback runs exactly in the offending window, which is the only way to reproduce a concurrency defect without concurrency; it is verified to fail with the `refresh()` removed.

**Une seule des huit cartes de glace est portée** (2026-09-06) — *Warmth* devient le sort **Chaleur** (`element: parchemin`, soin de 3 points, aucune mécanique neuve). Les sept autres restent des dettes **réécrites**, chacune nommant ce qui lui manque encore, parce que quatre cartes honnêtes valent mieux que huit approximatives. ⚠ Leur ancienne raison — « ni sort de froid, rien à quoi résister » — était **périmée** depuis que *Morsure de Froid* a donné sa première source au froid ; ce qui bloque réellement l'*Anneau de Chaleur* et le *Bracelet de Glace* est plus subtil : leur promesse dominante est l'immunité aux **coffres de glace et rivières gelées**, or un dégât de terrain ne porte **aucun `type_degat`**, donc `resistance_degats_type` ne l'intercepterait pas sans un second changement. *Chill* et *Ice Storm* butent ailleurs : leurs jumeaux Dread confirment une cible **multiple**, et `MotsClesSort::CIBLE_MONSTRES_ZONE` est explicitement `NON_IMPLEMENTES` côté héros. **Une dette dont la raison a changé doit être réécrite** : une dette périmée ment aussi sûrement qu'une clé décorative.

⚠ **La dette du 2026-09-06 s'est elle-même périmée avant d'avoir vécu deux jours** (audit du 2026-09-10, `docs/plan-glace-et-degats-mind.md`). *Gel de l'Esprit* était déjà porté le jour où le texte ci-dessus disait « Mind Freeze, non porté » sur le Brassard de Glace, et `ResolveurTour::saignerParTerrain()`/`saignerSurRiviere()` blessaient déjà quand le texte disait « aucun lecteur n'applique `terrains.effet` en jeu ». La règle de ce fichier s'applique donc à elle-même : trois cartes en sont ressorties portées. **Orbe Céleste** (Sky Orb, *Mage of the Mirror*) absorbe 4 points de dégâts de Mind un jeton à la fois — `MoteurSorts::absorbePartielDegatMind()`, une méthode sœur d'`absorbeDegat()` qui grignote un MONTANT plutôt que de bloquer une NATURE entière, puisque *Gel de l'Esprit* ne porte aucun `type_degat`. **Anneau de Chaleur** (Ring of Warmth) est porté sur `immunite_degat: froid`, désormais SANS charge (immunité permanente, comme tout objet sans compteur) — les deux terrains que sa carte nomme portent enfin leur `type_degat` (`TerrainSeeder`), lu par `absorbeDegat()` aux deux points de passage qui blessent par le sol. **Raquettes de Vitesse** (Snowshoes of Speed) est portée sur `Equipement::bonusDeplacementActif()`, symétrique permanent de `malusDeplacement()`, borné au thème de boîte FIGÉ du groupe faute d'une définition plus précise de « région gelée » dans la source. ⚠ **Brassard de Glace** (Armband of Ice) reste NON porté : sa moitié terrain est désormais atteignable (même mécanisme que l'Anneau de Chaleur) mais deux clauses sur quatre manquent toujours un lecteur propre — l'immunité à *Gel de l'Esprit* SPÉCIFIQUEMENT (rien n'exclut un porteur de `MoteurDread::cibleMindFreeze()`) et la réduction d'1 point sur *Ice Storm* côté héros (ciblage de zone `NON_IMPLEMENTES`). Porter ce bracelet sur sa seule moitié terrain aurait vendu une immunité au gel qui laisse geler par Gel de l'Esprit — la carte à moitié tenue que ce fichier existe pour ne jamais afficher.
