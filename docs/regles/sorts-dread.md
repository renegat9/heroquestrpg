# Sorts de Dread — la magie du MJ

> Extrait de `CLAUDE.md` le 2026-09-06, **verbatim**. Ce sont les **règles en
> vigueur** — pas un document daté comme `docs/plan-*.md` / `docs/verdict-*.md`.
> Index : `CLAUDE.md` §« Règles établies ».

**Dread spells now obey the players' own rules** (2026-09-01). The GM's magic was decided by `MoteurDread::choisirSort()` on a target list that was simply *every standing hero in the quest*: no line of sight, no range, no room. A boss struck a hero on the far side of the dungeon, through walls, inside a room never opened — the exact inverse of the rule the engine enforces on heroes ("line of sight required for EVERY spell"). `ciblesEnVue()` now filters through `FabriqueGrille::pour()` with `figuresBloquent: true`, the **same** call `MoteurSorts::filtrerLigneDeVue()` makes: one rule, both sides, or the MJ is playing a different game. ⚠ **Fuite is the exception** and keeps the full list — fleeing the only heroes you can *see* would be jumping into the arms of the one a wall was hiding; Charge likewise needs a path, not a sightline.

⚠ **`sorts_dread.palier` had no reader at all.** Doc 09 §4 states it as a *minimum* tier ("les sous-boss lancent déjà les sorts mineurs, le boss final ajoute les sorts vilains") and nothing enforced it: a sub-boss could Command, Summon and Flee. `sortsDisponibles()` gates on it, which is also what makes a **named archetype scale with the figure that carries it** — the Chamane Gobelin (sous-boss) draws Frayeur/Sommeil/Trait de Chaos from `chaman_orque`, a boss-rank shaman would also get Commandement, and `config/archetypes_lanceurs.php` stays declared *complete*.

⚠ **Two priorities decided on facts they never checked.** Tempête de feu was ranked first on the count of heroes *standing in the quest* while it only ever burns the caster's cell + 4 orthogonals — so it was chosen, journalled and spent on a zone holding nobody; `casesZone()`/`ciblesDansZone()` are now the single point of passage for both the choice and the resolution. And the control branch tested its three spells against **one** hero, the globally weakest Mind: if he already carried the condition the boss gave up on control entirely, though a companion beside him was perfectly sleepable. `cibleControle()` is now shared by `choisirSort()` and `sortDreadControle()` — the same recurring lesson as `Salles::indexDe()`: two copies of a rule too simple to notice drifting. Trait de Chaos also picked `$cibles->first()` (id order — the group's founder, all campaign long) where its own comment promised "le plus proche / le plus faible"; it now sorts on distance then HP. ⚠ That spell was **deleted on 2026-09-04** (no card), but the reader survives it under its own name, `cibleOffensive()`, and still serves every single-target damage spell.

⚠ **The `invocation` CAPACITY had no reader either** — the Seigneur and the Liche declared it and only the *spell* ever summoned. It is wired on the `charge` seam, costs **no** Dread usage (it is a capacity, not a spell), **shares** the spell's once-per-encounter lock so a monster carrying both summons once, and never fires **in contact**: a boss ringed by two heroes calling for reinforcements instead of striking is a gift, not a threat (same arbitration `pondre()` already makes for Spawn). `aCapacite()` reads **keys as well as values**, since `monstres.capacites` is a list for bare capacities and a map for parameterised ones (`['spawn' => ['creature' => …]]`) — reading values alone made every parameterised capacity invisible to it, the trap `pondre()` was already working around with its own `data_get()`.

⚠ **A live quest immediately found one more, and it was mine**: Trait de Chaos was sorted with `->sortBy([$closureA, $closureB])`, and Laravel's array form treats a callable as a **comparator** called `$f($a, $b)`, not as a key extractor — a one-argument closure therefore returned *the distance of `$a`* as the comparison result, and the boss struck the hero it was furthest from. It never errored. One closure returning a couple, and a test that only proves anything when the **nearer** hero comes first in the collection: staged the other way round, the vacuous version passed on the broken code too.

⚠ **The same `sortBy` had a TWIN, and grepping for the pattern found it**: `ResolveurTour::tirerSiCibleEnVue()` chose a ranged monster's target the same way — so the archer took the **robust** hero while a companion stood at 1 PV, the exact inverse of the comment above it ("PV de Body les plus faibles (achever), puis la plus proche"). One bug class, two sites: `sortBy([$f, $g])` is never a multi-key sort. On **two** candidates the buggy form returns the SECOND, whichever it is — so the locking test only proves something with the hero to finish off **first** in the collection, and it asserts that ordering rather than trusting it.

⚠ **The usage counters were in `Cache::forever`, and the consolidated rule forbids it.** A lost key returns 0, so the boss silently stops casting for the rest of the quest — undiagnosable at the table. They are columns now (`usages_dread`, `invocation_dread_utilisee`, `fuite_dread_utilisee`), which also puts them **in the snapshot**: a `/reprise` no longer hands the boss back the spells it had already spent. `brule` and `degat_differe` were missing from that snapshot for the same reason and went in with them — a restore used to put out the troll's burn. And `MoteurDread` kept **two occupancy loops of its own** (`grilleQuete()`, `casesLibresAdjacentes()`) that ignored furniture: a boss charged through a bookcase, Fuite teleported it into a table, and a summoned skeleton could land on a table or on a fallen hero. Both delegate to `FabriqueGrille::pour()`, the engine's single furniture loop.

**The DREAD SPELLS are a conversion too, and it cost us a spell** (René's photos, 2026-09-04 — `dread_spells.pdf`, 30 shots = **29 distinct spells**, *Channel Dread* printed twice). Fourth source switch after the armoury (2026-08-15), the spells (2026-09-02) and the artefacts (2026-09-03), and the first one that **removed** more than it added: the **Trait de Chaos** existed on no card. It was ours, it carried the whole "priority 2" of the spell choice, and it left by migration — `SortDreadSeeder` writes with `updateOrCreate` and never purges, exactly like the five cardless artefacts the day before. "The cards are the source" is only a property if it subtracts too, and `SortsDreadSourcesTest` now checks the registry **both ways**. **23 of the 29 are ported**; the six others are registered in `config/cartes.php` (section `dread`, exposed by `/api/guide`) each naming the mechanic it waits on — Mind damage and "shock" (*Mind Freeze*), hero→werewolf transformation (*Werewolf's Curse*), destructible terrain with a counter (*Ice Wall*), a movement mode for a monster (*Skate*), and — the same missing mechanic for both — **a monster acting outside the monster phase** (*Dispel*, *Mirror Magic*): `MoteurReactions` only ever talks to heroes, posting an offer on a private channel and waiting for a phone.

⚠ **The choice is by MECHANIC, never by spell NAME** — that recabling was the precondition, and it is the talents' lesson one level up. `choisirSort()` recognised its seven spells by `nom` ("Tempête de feu", "Trait de Chaos", "Sommeil"…); with 22 spells a name cascade would have done exactly what it did to the skill tree — fifteen spells seeded, never chosen, without an error anywhere. `sortUtilisable()` is now the hard filter (empty zone, corridor ban, nobody to heal, lock spent) and `scoreSort()` the ranking by family; ties fall back to the **repertoire order**, which `sortsDisponibles()` now actually preserves (`whereIn` returned catalogue order, so the preference declared in `config/archetypes_lanceurs.php` governed nothing).

⚠ **Resistance changed NATURE, and that is the deepest change.** Not one card in the deck grants a Mind roll **at cast time**: the spell lands, and it is its **continuation** that is contested. Five say it word for word — "broken immediately or on a future turn by the hero rolling 1 red die for each of their Mind Points; if a 6 is rolled, the spell is broken" (*Sleep*, *Command*, *Fear*, *Cloud of Dread*, *Mind Blast*) — and *Dreadlights* changes both numbers: **one** die, threshold **5-6**, and it never mentions Mind at all. Conflating the two would invert the spell, freeing a wizard (Mind 4) fast and a barbarian (Mind 1) almost never. Hence two keywords, `rupture_6_par_mind` and `rupture_5_6_un_de`, the same word and the same reader as the monster side has had since 2026-09-02. The roll is taken **at once**, then **at the opening of every turn** — `ResolveurTour::ouvrirTourDesHeros()`, the only "start of turn" a round-based engine owns. ⚠ Our *Sommeil* was a `jet_mind` at cast: it could fail outright and, once posted, never lifted on its own — a sleeper who never wakes is a player who puts the phone down.

⚠ **A duration and a rupture are two exits for one condition, and they raced.** `poserConditionHeros()` took an `int` where "0 = use the catalogue default", which made "**no counter**" inexpressible — precisely what a rupture-ended condition needs. *Paralysé* carries `duree_defaut: 3` for the heroes' Hypnotic Flame; the *Nuée d'Effroi* only ends on a 6, and a three-turn counter would have lifted it behind the card's back. The parameter is `?int` now. Same defect, sharper: **`perd_prochain_tour` (Étourdi) had no reader at all** since the table was created — a stunned hero played normally — and *Tempest* gives it both a producer and a reader at once; but with `duree_defaut: 1`, `decrementerDurees()` deleted the condition at end of round *just before* the turn opening came to read it. The turn was never lost. `duree_defaut: 0` is the fix, and it is the documented meaning: "no counter, the exit is a trigger".

⚠ **Three ports corrected a rule we had softened.** **Fear** says "may ONLY USE 1 Attack die" — a **ceiling**, not a malus: our `malus_des_attaque: 1` cost one die to a barbarian who throws five, and the ceiling was *already* written on the monster side (`terrifie` does `min($des, 1)` in `InstanceMonstre::apresConditions()`). Both sides of the table finally say the same thing; `des_attaque_max` applies **last**, on the total, which is the whole difference. **Firestorm** fills the **room** — "all heroes **and monsters** in the same room with the spellcaster; the spellcaster is unaffected; not used in corridors" — where ours swept the caster's cell plus four orthogonals with combat dice: neither the right zone nor the right resolution, and it ignored monsters entirely. **Summon Undead** rolls a **d6 table** ("1-2 = 4 skeletons; 3-4 = 3 skeletons, 2 zombies; 5-6 = 2 zombies, 2 mummies") where ours conjured two skeletons, full stop — the table is what separates a reinforcement from a swing in the fight, and it is what makes the spell worth a boss tier.

⚠ **Three cards needed no new mechanic, only a reader that already existed.** *Lightning Bolt* reuses `App\Partie\Rayon`, written for the Éclair scroll — same sentence on both cards, word for word. *Ball of Flame* is the hero card of the same name seen from the other side of the table: `degats_fixes` + `des_rouges`, one rule, both sides. *Reanimation* — "reanimate all defeated skeletons, zombies, or mummies in the same room" — needed nothing at all: our defeated instances stay in the DB with their position, there was only ever standing them back up. It is the one spell in the deck that makes a victory reversible, and its **only** guard is the once-per-encounter lock; ⚠ it is deliberately **exempt** from the no-contact rule the other summons obey, because its precondition (corpses on this floor) already means the party fought here — refusing it in contact would make it unplayable.

⚠ **`Étreinte des Ronces` (Creeping Grasp) woke two dead keys at once.** *Immobilisé* had lived in the catalogue since the table was created **with no producer**, and its `fin: liberation` **with no reader**. The card supplies both: a skull on one combat die costs 1 Body and pins the hero, and "the targeted hero **or another adjacent hero** can spend an action to destroy the vines" is the new `liberer_entraves` option — cut on `soin_allie`'s pattern, one option carrying its targets, the list being the whitelist the resolver re-validates. It clears **every** condition bearing `deplacement_interdit`, by mechanic and not by name, so a second entangling card will have nothing to rewire.

⚠ **The Dread magic was MUTE in the combat log.** `sort_dread` had no case in `JournalCombat::ligneType()` and fell to `default => []`: the boss cast, a hero lost HP, and the fil du combat said not a word — the same defect as the walked-on trap of 2026-08-05, breaking the same rule (an automatic effect nothing announces is unplayable). It renders now, and so do the two turn-opening payloads, which **ride up into `resultat.tour_monstres.actions`** by reference: journalled to the DB they would still be invisible to the manette, which only reads the request's result — and it is that roll which decides whether the sleeper wakes.

⚠ **The payload is unified on `resultats[]`**, one entry per victim even when there is only one. A control spell can now take a whole room, and two payload shapes for one family always drift — `sortDreadDegats()` was flat while `tempeteDeFeu()` already used a list. Per-victim keys (`cible`, `degats`, `effet_applique`, `contresort`, `rupture_immediate`, `absorbe`) moved inside it.

⚠ **A third palier was born: `base`.** The extensions give magic to **ordinary** creatures — the Dread Cultist, the Specter and the Blightweaver are tier `base` and each cast their spells "1× per quest" (doc 18) — and `reinitialiserUsagesInstance()` only ever armed sous-boss and boss. A base monster **with a repertoire** now gets one usage; testing the tier alone left them mute, and testing the repertoire alone would have handed three usages to a goblin the AI happened to dress as a shaman. The Specter's "at will" is bounded to that one usage, and the divergence is stated rather than hidden.

⚠ **The repertoires are no longer invented.** `config/archetypes_lanceurs.php` now transcribes the official casters documented in doc 18, box by box — nine archetypes, each with a **carrier** in the bestiary (`SortsDreadSourcesTest` checks that both ways: an archetype nobody carries is the decorative config this project hunts everywhere else). The Frozen Horror is the honest edge case: its card names six spells of which **three are not ported**, so its repertoire keeps the three that are plus one of the "6 at the GM's choice" the card grants it — naming an absent spell would shrink the boss in silence, since `sortsDisponibles()` filters on what exists.

⚠ **Rust is the one spell whose effect OUTLIVES the quest** (René's call, 2026-09-04: "c'est correct qu'un joueur puisse perdre un objet"). No condition, no rupture, no duration — the piece leaves the inventory for good, and `Equipement::recalculerCombat()` follows, the same passage the thrown weapon uses (`des_attaque` is a **column** kept in step at equip time, not a live computation; deleting the row without recomputing would leave the hero swinging a sword he no longer owns). ⚠ It changed what **`objets.metallique`** means: the column marked only armour, and now marks **every piece whose material a reader can act on** — swords, axes and bracers included. The rule is "flag it when a reader can act on that fact", which is why metal *artefact* weapons stay unflagged (Rust exempts artefacts, and nothing else looks at a weapon's material). ⚠ Both historic readers are therefore **scoped to `categorie === 'armure'`**: the Bard's card says "wearing no METAL ARMOR", the Druid's and the Rogue's "may not wear metal armor" — without the scope, flagging swords would have taken every metal weapon from two classes, an interdiction no card pronounces, born of a column that changed reach. One stated divergence, and it is small: the card enumerates "sword or helmet", our catalogue has no notion of *sword*, and a battle axe rusts exactly as a longsword does — so it reads "a **metal** piece in hand or on the head". Staff, wand and crossbow are wood: immune without a single exception written for them.

⚠ **`Morsure de Froid` gives `TypeDegat::FROID` its first SOURCE**, which was declared `SANS_SOURCE` until now. Stated consequence rather than a hidden one: the Armband of Ice and the Ring of Warmth become portable — they are not ported, and stay in the card registry. The ice box stays out by René's earlier call; what changed is that the reason is no longer "nothing to resist".

⚠ **The hero side carried the mirror hole** (2026-09-02). `MoteurSorts` marked Concentration used in `Cache::forever` — durable per-quest state in cache, the same forbidden pattern, and *by construction* out of reach of the snapshot. It needed **no new column**: the three nodes carrying the mechanic (Concentration/Rappel/Communion) already declare `frequence: une_fois_par_quete`, so `Talents::disponible()`/`consommer()` and `etat_personnage_quete.capacites_utilisees` were the counter all along — it simply wasn't used here. ⚠ But moving it exposed the real defect: `Sauvegarde` serialized **only 7 keys** of a hero's quest state (position, slots, `tombe`), so a `/reprise` or a "Recommencer la quête" **handed back every once-per-quest capacity already spent**, wiped the spawnling tokens hooked onto the hero and refunded the first-blow defence die. `capacites_utilisees`, `styles_epuises`, `garde_tenace_utilisee` and `jetons_rejeton` now ride in both directions. The per-**turn** counters deliberately do not: the snapshot is taken at a round opening, exactly where they have just been reset. Nor does `reaction_en_attente` — a suspended round takes no snapshot, and resurrecting a stale offer would re-ask a question already answered. The `??` defaults on restore are not decorative: a snapshot taken before this date carries none of the four keys, and a campaign in flight must still resume.
