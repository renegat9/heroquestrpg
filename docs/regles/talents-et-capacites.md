# Talents et capacités — la grille 3×3 et les cartes de classe

> Extrait de `CLAUDE.md` le 2026-09-06, **verbatim**. Ce sont les **règles en
> vigueur** — pas un document daté comme `docs/plan-*.md` / `docs/verdict-*.md`.
> Index : `CLAUDE.md` §« Règles établies ».

**Card capacities of the extension classes are IN PLAY, not in the catalogue** (`App\Partie\CapacitesInnees`, `App\Partie\StylesElementaires`, doc 01 §4ter). The 17 capacities of the 6 classes that have them are wired, each to a real reader; Druide and Warlock have none — their cards are *spells*. They come free with the figure (`competences.innee`), and the counters are the point: `capacites_utilisees` (per quest, dies with the quest state), `capacites_tour` (per **turn**, cleared where `a_joue` is), `styles_epuises` (the Monk's third rhythm, which **recharges mid-quest**). Adding a "once per X" without wiring its counter turns it into "once per campaign" or into no limit at all — that is exactly what `bonus_des_attaque_flanc` did until its counter existed.

`ResolveurTour::frapper()` is the **single** strike core: menu attack, Furie, sweeping strike and out-of-turn riposte all go through it, because recopying the dice maths per entry is how a bonus ends up applying "in some places" (the seven-way defence split that `desDefenseHeros()` fixed). Range and line of sight stay with the CALLER — they don't mean the same thing on each path. `StylesElementaires::sourceActivable()` arbitrates the mechanics with **two** sources (`attaque_balayee` is both the Berserker's *Frénésie* and the Monk's *Œil du Cyclone*), so menu and resolver always answer the same question.

Three porting decisions, each stated where it is taken: the **Furie** caps at `pv_body - 1` (a capacity promising an *immediate* attack cannot fell its bearer first); **Ambidextrie**'s second strike was resolved by porting the off-hand slot itself, so the dagger is now really held; **Parler à la Pierre** grants an automatic search, because our "Fouiller la zone" already searches traps AND secret doors in one action (doc 14 §3.1) — the card's promise is our default, and a technique that costs a Style to do what everyone gets free is a rule promised and never kept. The Monk's recovery is taken **literally** — no monster *in his line of sight* at the start of his turn, not "the dungeon is clear" — and the Fire lock does the rest: his strongest strike only arrives after a real fight. ⚠ A technique played **out of turn** (*Torrent Tournoyant*) exhausts its element but does **not** eat the coming turn's activation: "once per turn" governs what you do on YOUR turn.

Every one of them is **visible**: a swept strike announces itself in the combat log before its volley, the Explorer's trap warning prints a line (Zargon warns out loud at the table — but no tile is placed, the trap stays `cache`), and each new option type carries its own icon on the controller. An automatic effect nothing announces is unplayable — same rule as the spawnling tokens.

**Talents are a GRID: 3 columns × 3 rows per class** (`competences.categorie` / `colonne` / `rang`, `App\Engine\MotsClesTalent`, `App\Partie\Talents`, doc 01 §6 — René's call, 2026-08-23). A column is a **category named by the class itself** (« Furie » for the barbare, « Traque » for the explorateur — free labels, no shared vocabulary), and buying row *n* requires row *n−1* **of the same column**. `prerequis_id` keeps its meaning and is now **computed** from `(classe, colonne, rang − 1)` instead of named by hand, which makes a chain across two columns structurally impossible and leaves `CompetenceController::acquerir()` untouched. Nine cells for the 4-7 points a campaign yields: you go down a column and give up another. The three nodes of a column are **different effects of the same domain**, not one number growing — the chain is an order of acquisition, not a power curve. The 17 `innee` card capacities stay **outside** the grid (`colonne`/`rang`/`categorie` null): they come with the figure and cost no point, and putting them in a cell would claim otherwise. ⚠ **They live in the same pivot as purchased nodes, and the point formula counted them** — `pointsCompetence()` did `(niveau − 1) − competences()->count()`, so at level 2 the berserker (3 innate) and the monk (4) had `max(0, 1 − 3)` and `max(0, 1 − 4)`, i.e. **zero points**, while the druid — one of the only two classes with no innate capacity — got his normally (René, 2026-09-04, after the third quest). The berserker would have waited for level 4 and the monk for level 5 to open their first node, with no screen saying why: free capacities were being charged for. The count is now scoped to `innee = false`, and `AuthController` — which carried **its own copy** of the formula, with the same defect — delegates instead. The former « barbare alone at 4 nodes » debt is settled by construction.

⚠ **The grid was sized for a progression the engine never delivered** (René, 2026-09-04: « pourquoi on a dû attendre 3 quêtes avant de passer de niveau »). Doc 01 §5 declares **three** level-up triggers — each sub-boss, the final boss, and « certains **objectifs de quête majeurs** marqués par le gabarit » — and `MonteeNiveau::JALONS` read only the first two. The third had **no reader at all**, so a campaign yielded 1/2/3/4/5 levels by length against the « ~5 to 8 per campaign » the same section targets, and against the nine cells « for four to seven points » of §6: at two points you cannot even reach rank 3 of a single column, so the choice that was meant to hurt never arose. ⚠ **The marker and the cadence are two distinct halves, and both are needed.** The **gabarit** says which objectives are major (`structure.objectif_majeur` — the doc's « marqués par le gabarit »); the **arc** says which of those quests count (`App\Partie\CadenceNiveaux`). Without the second half a very long campaign would grant a level on each of its fifteen ordinary quests — **twenty levels** for a band that tops out at eight; the doc asks for it in as many words (« la cadence exacte se cale sur l'arc selon la longueur »). Positions are spread evenly over the ordinary quests, never onto a milestone (one quest never levels twice), and the total lands in 5-8 for any arc with ≥ 5 quests; below that the band is unreachable and **every quest levels**, which is the most that can be given. ⚠ It is a **placement, not a draw** — it depends only on the campaign plan and the quest count, both frozen at creation, so restarting a quest or reloading a snapshot never moves a major objective. ⚠ The objective must be **accomplished**, not merely survived: `quitter_donjon` also opens on a fully cleared dungeon (the anti-softlock fallback), and a level granted there would reward leaving rather than succeeding. ⚠ `quetes.objectif_majeur` is a **column stamped at start**, like `type_jalon` and for the same reason: the campaign plan is written by an async job, so re-deriving the cadence at quest end would let a quest begun before the plan landed change its own answer mid-flight. It rides the snapshot, and the journal names **which** of the three triggers fired.

⚠ **Talents are read by MECHANIC, never by node NAME** — that recabling was the precondition of the whole rework, and it repaired fifteen mute nodes. The engine used to look for `'Garde tenace'`, `'Coup puissant'`, `'Intimidation'`, `'Réserve arcanique'`, `'Concentration'`, `'Désamorçage'`, `'Tir précis'`, `'Œil du mineur'`, `'Contresort'`, `'Forge'`; the extension classes carried the **right** `effet.mecanique` under other names — *Esquive*, *Garde haute*, *Écorce*, *Refrain vaillant*, *Prestance*, *Méditation*, *Cartographe*, *Bras d'acier*, *Coup sauvage*, *Second couplet*, *Rappel*, *Communion*, *Doigts de fée*… — and **did nothing at all**, while `CompetenceSeeder` promised in comment to use "only mechanics that already have a reader". True of the mechanic, false of the wiring. `App\Partie\Talents` is now the single reader (`a()`, `noeud()`, `valeur()`, `disponible()`, `consommer()`), and `CapacitesInnees` extends it — a card capacity is just one competence among others. ⚠ `noeud()` takes **criteria** on `effet`, and that is not cosmetic: `bonus_des_attaque` names both the permanent bonus already banked in the column *and* the Frénésie that only counts under half HP; reading them undistinguished applies one for the other. Two **class** gates fell with the recabling (`magicien` for Concentration, `nain`/`explorateur` for disarming): owning the node is what decides now, and a `desamorcer_piege` talent **grants** the right rather than only softening the failure.

⚠ **Three locks keep talents from being decorative, and all three are automated.** (1) The **text**: every node carries a hand-written `description` (the gain, its condition, its cadence) *and* an `avantage` **derived from `effet`** by `MotsClesTalent::avantage()` — never typed, so it cannot disagree with the mechanic. Both are published (`/api/competences`, `/api/guide`) and shown side by side on the talent sheet; a test demands ≥ 25 characters, the number when `effet.valeur` exists, and the cadence when `effet.frequence` does. The display vocabulary lives **server-side** on purpose: `store/game.js` held its own table keyed on *column* names (`pv_body_max`), so a competence `effet` produced **no chip at all** — every talent showed without a single figure and nobody had noticed. (2) The **registry**: `MotsClesTalent::MECANIQUES` maps each mechanic to `lecteur` + `libelle` + `icone`, is checked **both ways** against the seeder, and — the assertion that was missing — each declared reader's class and method must exist *and* at least one of them must **name the key in its own file**. `CapacitesInnees` used to declare its readers with nothing verifying the link, and four of them (`plancher_pv`, `annule_degats`, `annule_degats_voisin`, `defi_errant`) pointed at a file where the word never appears — the key lives as a constant in `App\Engine\ReactionEffet`. "At least one" and not "all", because some sites *apply* a rule without naming it: `Grille::autoriserFranchissement()` only receives a boolean. (3) The **proof in play**: `TalentsEnJeuTest` exercises one case per mechanic through the real engine and real routes, `TalentsRecablesTest` replays every recabled reader **on a class that is not the historic name**. Corollary, assumed: a mechanic without a case there is not seeded — better a cell reusing a proven effect than a talent that does nothing.

⚠ **Twenty-one new mechanics came with the grid, each with its reader** on an existing seam: `ResolveurTour::frapper()` (`bonus_des_attaque_distance`, `bonus_des_attaque_contre_tier` — read from the **catalogue** `tier`, never the AI-dressed name —, `bonus_des_attaque_apres_deplacement` — gated on `a_deplace`, since `deplacement_tour` is fixed at menu generation and an immobile hero looked like he had sprinted —, `ignore_defense_monstre`, `attaque_supplementaire_apres_kill`, `inflige_condition_sur_touche`), `MoteurDegats::infligerAHeros()` (`reduction_degats`, applied **after** the interception listener so a cancelled blow still reaches zero, and never on `SOURCE_SACRIFICE`), `MoteurSorts::absorbeDegat()` (`resistance_degats_type`, read **before** any charged item so a permanent talent never spends one), `ResolveurTour::resoudreAttaqueMonstre()` (`bonus_des_defense_contre_distance`, `bonus_des_defense_allie_adjacent` — neighbours only, never its bearer —, `malus_des_monstre_adjacent`), `ResolveurTour::sortDegats()` (`bonus_degats_sort`, via `ResultatAttaque::avecDegatsAjoutes()` so `degats`/`pvBodyApres`/`cibleTombee` recompute in one place, and never on a fully parried spell), `MoteurSorts` (`regain_sort`, reusing `App\Engine\RegainEffet` rather than a second grammar, and rendering **one** spell; `sacrifice_pv_pour_sort`, refused at 1 PV), `MoteurPortes::detecterSecretesAdjacentes()`, `ResolveurTour::tronquerSurRacines()`/`tronquerSurChausseTrappes()` (`ignore_terrain_entravant`), `Quete::aFouille()` (`fouille_supplementaire`, extra entries suffixed `#n` because entries are de-duplicated), `RareteButin` via the searcher's level (`rarete_butin_amelioree` — one curve advanced, not a second invented), `PhaseMarche::ouvrir()` (`remise_marche`, the group's **best** discount and not their sum, since the purse is shared), `relance_jet_mind_rate`, and a new action option `soin_allie` (1d6 to a hero in contact, which **stands them back up**; `option.parametres.cibles` **is** the whitelist the resolver re-validates). ⚠ Adding a mechanic means writing its reader, its registry line and its in-play test **before** the data — the same rule the armoury and the spell vocabulary already obey.
